# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Tollgate to be a respectful, harassment free space for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Anything else a reasonable person would call unprofessional.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people represent this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to hello@dipenpatel.dev. Reports are kept private. The maintainer can edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block someone from the project for behavior that breaks this code.
